04Method

Each result that a conclusion depends on is computed twice, by two different methods. Where they disagree, the disagreement is the finding.

  1. Two independent routesA closed form against a numerical solver, a continuum model against an atomistic one, a second code written from the paper rather than from the first.
  2. Negative results stay publicSix of the repositories end in a negative or limiting result. Withdrawn claims are kept on the record next to the numbers that produced them.
  3. Stated assistanceAI coding assistants are used for development and adversarial review. Each repository states how its results were checked; responsibility for every claim is mine.
